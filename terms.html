The service

Inoa Games is a collection of browser games by Inoa Services. Guests can play on one device; an account saves progress across devices. Free accounts have 3 game starts per day (a start opens a 30-minute play session of one game; the count resets at midnight UTC).

Accounts

You must be 13 or older to create an account, or be a parent or guardian creating it for your child. Keep your password private. One person per account. We may suspend accounts that abuse the service (for example, automating play or attacking the site). Suspension doesn't delete your data.

Invite friends

Signed-in players get a personal invite link. When someone creates an account through your link and confirms their email, you earn a US$1.00 credit, up to 5 credits (US$5.00) per calendar month (UTC). Credits are saved now and apply only toward a future Inoa Games subscription. They have no cash value and can't be withdrawn, sold or transferred, and they end if you delete your account. Each new account counts once, and inviting yourself doesn't count. Credits from fake, duplicate or automated accounts may be removed.
