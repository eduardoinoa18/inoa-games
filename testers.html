Tester guide

Thank you for testing! Ten minutes on your own phone tells us more than any lab. Everything is free during the beta and no account is needed.

1. Play on your phone (5 minutes)

2. Your phone as a controller (3 minutes, needs a second screen)

  1. Open a game on a TV, laptop or tablet and tap 📱 Phones on its title screen. A QR code and a 4-letter code appear.
  2. On your phone, scan the QR code (or open the hub's 📷 Scan, or type the code at the pad page).
  3. Tap Allow on the big screen when your phone asks to join.
  4. For racing and action games the controller turns sideways: hold your phone like a game controller. Use ↻ at the top if you prefer it upright.
  5. Tell us: did it connect on the first try? Did the controls respond right away (⚡ means a direct connection, ☁ means relayed)?

3. Send feedback (1 minute)

Use 💬 Feedback in any game's ⚙️ settings, at the end of a round, or on the hub. Pick a face, add a note if you like, and send. It automatically includes your phone type and how smoothly the game was running, so you don't need to describe your device.

Open the games

Feedback stores your note, the game, your screen size, a coarse device type (for example "android chrome") and the frame rate. No name or email unless you're signed in. See the privacy page.
