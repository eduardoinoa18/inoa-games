FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open the link and play. On a phone, add it to your home screen for a full-screen app. iOS and Android apps of the same game come in Phase 3.

Is it for kids or adults?

Both. The hub is all ages: Snack Squad Safari and the cozy games are built for families, while the signature 3D titles (Depths, Driftwood Builders, Tide Lines, Island Drift) are rated 10+ with ranked play and builds for adults. A child profile hides ranked play and voice. There is no text chat with strangers, no gore and no gambling mechanics. Emotes and quick-chat only; parties are private by code.

Is it hard?

Friendly is the default: unlimited lives, a checkpoint every 20 seconds, and a teammate can revive you in 5 seconds. Normal and Brutal are there for people who want a fight, and Brutal par times are made to be chased.

What do I actually pay for?

One $4.99 unlock for more worlds, arenas, classes and 8-player online rooms. Your guests never pay to join your party. Cosmetics are expression, never power, and every one is also earnable.

Where does my hero live?

On your account, synced across phone, laptop and app. Start as a guest, upgrade with Apple, Google or email later and nothing is lost.

Who makes this?

Inoa Services, a Methuen, MA company. Pixel Pact is the first game; the hub grows a game a month.
