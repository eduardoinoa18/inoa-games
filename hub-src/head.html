FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open the link and play in the browser.

Is it for kids or adults?

Both. Everything is all ages: no chat, no strangers, no gambling mechanics. Cozy games and Snack Squad Safari are built for families; Depths, Driftwood Builders, Tide Lines and Island Drift have harder options for older players.

Is it hard?

Friendly is the default. Many games have an optional harder mode for people who want a challenge.

Does it cost anything?

No. Inoa Games is free while it is in preview. There are no ads and no purchases.

Where does my hero live?

In this browser on this device (local storage). Clearing site data resets it. Nothing is sent to a server.

Who makes this?

Inoa Services, a Methuen, MA company. Pixel Pact is the first game; the hub grows a game a month.
