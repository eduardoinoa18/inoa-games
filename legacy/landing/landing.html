FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open the link and play. On a phone, add it to your home screen for a full-screen app-style experience.

How does multiplayer work?

Today: up to four people on one screen using keyboards, gamepads and touch, with bots filling empty seats. Online rooms with QR phone pairing are the next milestone.

Is it hard?

Friendly difficulty gives unlimited continues and softer enemies. Normal and Brutal are there when you want them. Match bots scale with the same setting.

Is it like Brawlhalla or Smash?

Match mode uses the same idea: damage raises your knockback, and falling off the stage costs a stock. Pixel Pact's heroes, stages and art are its own.

What devices are supported?

Any modern browser: iPhone, Android, iPad, Mac, Windows, Chromebook and TV browsers. A phone in landscape gives the widest view.
