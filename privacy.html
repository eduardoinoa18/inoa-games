Children

Many games are made for families. Accounts must be created by someone 13 or older, or by a parent or guardian for their child. Children under 13 can play every game as a guest without an account. [OWNER TO CONFIRM with counsel: COPPA approach before launch.] If you believe a child under 13 created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Service providers

Vercel (hosting), Supabase (accounts and database), Stripe (payments; card details are entered on Stripe's page), jsDelivr (game engine library) and Google Fonts (fonts). Your choices

You can edit your display name and language, download nothing is required, and delete your account at any time from Account → Delete account (you'll confirm your password). Deleting cancels Premium immediately.

Cookies and storage

We use browser local storage for your settings, progress cache and sign-in session, and the browser cache (service worker) so games work offline. We don't use advertising cookies.
