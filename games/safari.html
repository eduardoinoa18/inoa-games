Snack Squad Safari

Your buddy is hungry. Roll down the 3D aisle, catch the snacks that are safe for you, read the labels like a detective, and practice the rescue plan with a grown-up. Feed your buddy and watch it grow.

Snack Squad Safari teaches habits, not medicine. Always follow the action plan from your own doctor or allergist.

How it works

🥚 Your buddy rides in the cart. Catch snacks that are safe for you to feed it; it hatches and grows as you go.
🛒 Move left and right to catch safe food and dodge foods with your allergens. Mistakes just mean "let's look again".
📦 Packaged foods show a ?. Press Read label to open the ingredients and decide: safe or not safe.
🧑‍🍳 A grown-up can play the Scanner: hold the purple button (or Shift) to reveal label hints early.
🚨 Buddy Rescue: if an allergen slips through, your buddy needs you. Practice the 3-step plan: tell a grown-up, auto-injector, call 911. Calm and clear.
🎁 Every 10 safe snacks opens a Mystery Snack: a rare safe treat for your collection.
🎮 Keyboard: A/D or ← → to move, Space to read, Shift for Scanner. Touch: the big buttons.

Buddy Rescue!

Itchy mouth, hives, a swollen lip, trouble breathing: those are the signs. Stay calm and follow the plan, in order.

1
Tell a grown-up right away
Say: "I ate something and I feel sick."
2
Use the epinephrine auto-injector
Outer thigh, hold it there and count.
3
Call 911
Even if you feel better. Then lie down and wait.

Practice only. Your real plan comes from your doctor or allergist; grown-ups, keep two auto-injectors nearby.
